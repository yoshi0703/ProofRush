## Prior evidence and added calculations

- Meakins et al. already published daily zone series, between-zone differences, and the late shift at uMhlanga. The present analysis gives an exact site-specific lower bound for any fixed multiplier, enumerates sign reversals, and evaluates the effort/accuracy tradeoff of a random bag audit on the observed catches. Its success probability is purely design-based on those catches. [Original field study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rsma.2022.102421)
- Buoninsegni et al. already described a rise in winter swash litter at Spina. Here we compute the signs on the two spatial supports and bound the difference from the independently rounded density and area. [Original Italian study and Appendix A](https://doi.org/10.3390/w17152173)
- The sampling estimator is the classical inverse-inclusion-probability estimator. OSPAR's guidance, Lavelle's comparison of strandline belts and perpendicular strips, and the two primary studies document why sampling boundaries matter. Our exact budgets are specific to the field observations. No proof of worldwide priority or external peer review is available.
