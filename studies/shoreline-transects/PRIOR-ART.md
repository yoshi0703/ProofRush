# Independent source and priority review

Reviewed 27 November 2026. The source-supported contribution is an exact audit of one printed NOAA spreadsheet option, a three-design calculation for records without selection metadata, and a descriptive replay on two disjoint cohorts. The monitored beaches, cleanup mechanisms, and general unequal-probability sampling theory have substantial prior literature.

## What NOAA instructs

- The 2021 technical memorandum, printed p. 8, asks for a truly random set of four different 5 m transects, chosen before travel, from the twenty starts at 0, 5, …, 95 m along a 100 m site. It offers a NOAA-supplied list or four spreadsheet cells containing `=MROUND(RANDBETWEEN(0,95),5)`. On a collision it says to try again until four values are unique. The instruction preserves four distinct surveyed locations but does not specify which cells to recompute. Whole-batch rejection and retention of previously accepted labels are two explicit model interpretations. The generator of the NOAA-supplied lists and the actual retry conventions remain unverified. [Burgess et al. 2021, printed p. 8](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/33282/noaa_33282_DS1.pdf#page=8)
- The NOAA educator page, marked updated 15 August 2024, describes four or more transects and recommends choosing integers from 0 through 19 and multiplying by five. It also suggests a twenty-sided die and mixed bottle caps. Independent uniform raw draws completed to four distinct starts give a uniform four-set under either of the two retry models. The educator page leaves collisions unspecified, and the source does not say that its choices were published as an erratum or as a retrospective correction of the community archive. The 2013 standing-stock protocol already used a supplied random-number table for preselection, illustrating why the 2021 spreadsheet probability cannot be assigned to all protocol years. [NOAA educator instructions](https://marinedebris.noaa.gov/before-every-survey-safety-tips-and-transect-selection) [Lippiatt, Opfer and Arthur 2013, §2.7 and §7.4](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/2681)
- The outcome here is observable main-beach items at least 2.5 cm in their longest dimension, measured from water's edge to the back barrier. The archive has a separate file for the optional band behind the barrier. Items per fixed-width strip are different from items per square metre because distance from water to back barrier varies. NOAA allows removal after surveying the strips and requests documenting its extent. Verification for public release is a staff review and gives no evidence for the drawing procedure used on a particular visit. [Burgess et al. 2021, printed pp. 9–12 and 17–19](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/33282/noaa_33282_DS1.pdf)

## Exact check and mathematical priority

An independent Python rational-arithmetic pass enumerated all 4,845 four-sets. It counted integer preimages using `5 * ((draw + 2) // 5)` for draws in `range(96)`. Whole-batch weights are products of preimage counts divided by their sum over the four-sets. For successive sampling it summed, over the 24 orderings of each set, the product of each next label's weight divided by the total weight of the remaining labels. All three distributions summed to one.

| Four-set law | Each endpoint | Each interior strip | Expected naive mean / true mean when all items are at endpoints |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Uniform | 1/5 | 1/5 | 100% |
| Rejective (whole batch) | 89/669 = 13.30343797% | 1249/6021 = 20.74406245% | 66.51718984% |
| Successive (duplicate only) | 257570377/1993493502 = 12.92055262% | 3729416627/17941441518 = 20.78660526% | 64.60276312% |

The individual endpoint deficits are 6.70 and 7.08 percentage points from 20%. A rounded raw draw hits an endpoint with probability 3/96. Its probability of being present in a four-set is the corresponding value in the table. If endpoint and interior stratum means are different, the ordinary four-strip mean's expected bias equals half of (endpoint inclusion minus 0.20) times (endpoint mean minus interior mean). Thus even the sign of bias depends on the spatial pattern. The endpoint-only abundance case illustrates potential bias under a stipulated law. For independent pairs, multiplying the largest inner product among the three four-set laws by 151 gives 0.033258 expected exact repeats, allowing a different specified law on the two visits. The calculation does not justify a repetition benchmark conditional on inclusion when retaining visits depends on the draws.

Rejective and successive sampling are classical. Yu defines these same collision rules and proves that inclusion probabilities under the former are more uniform in the sense of majorization for identical drawing probabilities and sample sizes. Fog treats the corresponding Fisher and Wallenius urn laws. Horvitz–Thompson estimation already gives the single-visit design-unbiased mean when the design and all relevant inclusion probabilities are known. These works are mathematical precedents for the computation, including its direction of change. [Yu 2012, Bernoulli](https://doi.org/10.3150/10-BEJ337) [Fog 2008](https://www.agner.org/random/nchyp2.pdf) [Horvitz and Thompson 1952](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1952.10483446)

The note's simultaneous-estimability criterion is a short finite linear consequence. For arbitrary count-independent mechanisms assigning probabilities q_r(S) to observed subsets S, an unbiased estimator for every response vector exists exactly when for each unit u there are coefficients a_(u,S) with sum over S containing u of q_r(S) a_(u,S) = 1/N for every r. Necessity compares expected outputs on the all-zero data and a single unit's response spike; the conditional output difference vanishes for any S omitting that unit. Sufficiency follows by summing these coefficients times the observed responses. Conditioning by the number J of endpoints simplifies those equations by exchangeability. The endpoint formulation assumes a nonempty endpoint stratum, at least one interior observation under each design, a common fixed sample size, and no observed generator, draw order, or auxiliary metadata. With common sample size and within-stratum permutation invariance, the conditional law of the subset given J is uniform on its stratum-size class. These scope conditions matter for nonlinear and randomized estimators too. The three-design rank obstruction and evaluated bias tradeoffs are specific to this application. Priority for a general survey-sampling theory claim is unsupported.

## Related monitoring work and field scope

- Uhrin and Burgess's 2026 national survey paper explicitly reviews the participatory archive's site self-selection and uneven visit schedules, and describes a separate nationwide, spatially balanced design. It mentions random 5 m strip subsampling and cites the 2021 protocol. Its full accepted manuscript contains neither the rounding function nor a duplicate-redraw analysis. The national survey and associated power analyses do not validate the archive's historical within-site draws. The two cohorts in this note were independently extracted from disjoint ranks of the same frozen community site index. Both belong to that archive's filtered frame. [Uhrin and Burgess 2026, §§2–3](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/74440/noaa_74440_DS1.pdf) [Starcevich et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolind.2025.113807) [NOAA nationwide survey](https://marinedebris.noaa.gov/monitoring/establishing-marine-debris-baseline-us-through-nationwide-shoreline-survey)
- Prior field experiments already document litter turnover and the effects of visit timing, repeated removal, and observation conditions. NOAA's own experimental work addresses detection and survey methods, and its guide has consistency checks and explicitly warns that removal may influence future loads. The note's across-visit contrast is a diagnostic under a specified assignment design. It does not estimate how much a cleanup reduced later litter. Design-based inference under interference and specified exposure was established independently. Here previous assignment, previous removal, detection, and visit timing can differ, and no randomized validation linked strip selection to removals. All eleven pilot repeats came from one site, and both carryover and placebo intervals include zero. [Smith and Markic 2013](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0083694) [Brennan, Wilcox and Hardesty 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2018.06.352) [Haarr et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.marpolbul.2024.116720) [Burgess et al. 2024](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/56236) [Aronow and Samii 2017](https://doi.org/10.1214/16-AOAS1005)
- A targeted search for the literal formula, its DOI with correction/erratum terms, and NOAA transect endpoint rounding, collisions, and these numerical results did not locate an earlier public erratum or the same application-specific numerical calculation. The checked official and research sources include the original and current instructions, the 2013 precursor, the 2026 accepted nationwide-design manuscript, and the 2024 detection study. This search cannot establish chronological priority or rule out earlier internal analyses. The educator page itself is public prior art for obtaining uniform draws.

## Public data and redistribution

The program advertises public survey downloads via Export. The export's README describes the versions, fields and main-beach/back-barrier distinction, and the portal provides citation guidance. An explicit general redistribution license for the community-survey ZIPs or contributed material was not found in the inspected notes. The CC0 statement in the NOAA InPort record governs the separately named national-survey planning shapefile. The field guide also limits access to provided participant information and distinguishes verified public records from unverified private ones. [NOAA MDMAP program and Export instructions](https://marinedebris.noaa.gov/our-work/monitoring/marine-debris-monitoring-and-assessment-project) [MDMAP public data portal](https://mdmap.orr.noaa.gov/about) [InPort 73092, distribution and access sections](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/73092) [2021 field guide, printed pp. 12 and 16](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/33282/noaa_33282_DS1.pdf)

An archive of original code, aggregate outputs, exact proofs, data citation and checksums supports release while readers source record-level inputs from NOAA. Use download filters and manifest hashes to distinguish the frozen computation from a later portal export, which can change as records are edited or new ones are added. Public site or survey IDs in a numeric extract leave it linkable to the underlying archive even when names, notes, coordinates and photos are omitted.
