# Research record

The [preprint](paper.pdf) and its [source](paper.tex) concern Althöfer's alternating game on positive odd integers. They classify every position where the second player can force a win on the second move, give finite strategies for explicit further positions, and establish a lower bound on the Hausdorff dimension of both outcome closures in the 2-adic integers. The bound holds after independently prescribing any single residue class modulo a power of 3. These conclusions leave the sponsor's universal optimal-play question open. No prize claim or submission was made. External peer review remains pending.
