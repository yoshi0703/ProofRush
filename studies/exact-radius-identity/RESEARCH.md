## Primary comparisons and scope

Canonne's question asks which features of an individual known law determine testing difficulty at the exact radius. Earlier work bounds sample size using truncations at different radius multiples, or bounds radius up to constants at fixed sample size. Neither type of bound by itself determines the transition at a prescribed suffix mass. Our examples leave truncated summaries and other statistics available for the general problem. [Canonne](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v247/canonne24a.html) · [Valiant and Valiant](https://theory.stanford.edu/~valiant/papers/instanceOptFull.pdf) · [Blais, Canonne and Gur](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.CCC.2017.28) · [Chhor and Carpentier](https://doi.org/10.4171/MSL/32)

The ICALP 2018 proceedings explicitly define half-L1 TV and an inclusive alternative and state all-parameter sample optimality. The sufficient upper bound and its small-distance optimality survive the endpoint correction. The counterexample at one observation works for every known distribution at the stated radius and confidence, and a strictly positive null can have a nonempty alternative there. A separate comparison covered the authors' full version. [Proceedings](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2018.41) · [Full version](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~ecprice/papers/uniformity.pdf)
