# Value dimension of a position-free softmax attention layer

Daniel Hsu and Mingyue Xu posted *Attention-based representations for multi-task computation* on 4 August 2026. The paragraph following Theorem 3 asks whether the dimension of the value vectors in their symmetric-function theorem can be improved at intermediate readout degrees. Section 6 asks how much dimension is necessary. The object is one fixed-query attention layer whose heads are summed, followed by a polynomial threshold of degree \(D\). Parameters may depend on the length and on the individual Boolean target. [Original question and Theorem 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04243v1#S3.SS2) · [Discussion](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04243v1#S6)

For \(H,D\ge1\), write \(r\) for the least positive integer \(j\) with \(\binom{D+j}{j}\ge HD+1\). The least universal value dimension \(d_*(H,D)\) satisfies

\[
\min(D,r)/3\ \le\ d_*(H,D)\ \le\ \min(D,32r).
\]

At \(H\ge4^D\) and \(D\ge3\), some symmetric target of threshold degree at most \(HD\) escapes every representation with \(D-2\) value channels, although \(D\) channels suffice for every such target. This rules out a uniform improvement to \(o(\min(D,\log H))\). The joint bounds also describe parameter regimes with smaller sufficient dimension. The source's exact tradeoff between head count and threshold degree and its constructive additive bases are prior results. The new part is the dimension obstruction and the resulting matching order of the worst-case dimension. Exact integer dimensions and parity-specific necessity remain open. The theorem concerns discrete Boolean targets and one attention layer. [Paper](paper.pdf) · [Formal dimension theorem](lean/JointDimension.lean)

## Derivation and attempts to disprove the argument

- The coordinator and an independent agent represented all possible readouts by a polynomial after clearing one positive denominator. Counting strict sign profiles with Warren's theorem gives a finite separating Hamming grid. Another proof uses algebraic dependence among normalized polynomial coefficients. Its separating intervals force all representing roots into a box disjoint from the coefficient relation. The finite-grid step is essential because target-specific parameters may change as the sequence length changes. The second argument is formalized. [Formal lower bound](lean/AttentionLower.lean) · [Independent literature and lower-bound audit](verification/prior-work.md)
- The initial parameter count allowed one redundant coefficient of the common denominator. The proof uses that larger chart and still obtains the advertised bounds. Both definitions of the sign at zero were checked by shifting only the constant coefficient on a finite grid. Symmetry is imposed on the target's sign. Its witnessing multivariate polynomial need not be symmetric. [Finite-cube averaging](lean/Symmetry.lean) · [Weak and strict signs](lean/ReadoutBarrier.lean)
- Hsu and Xu's additive-basis lemma gives the elementary upper construction. Choosing a radix and a number of digits from the binomial threshold matches the lower order. An independent proof groups one real root per sign change and implements all coordinates with the same collection of heads. A sharper, paper-only corollary uses maximal rank for general rational curves and yields \(d_*\le\min(D,\max(3,r))\). An independent reviewer checked the real-coefficient and positive-denominator restrictions. The finite full-rank example has \(H=30,D=6\), four value channels, and rank 181 over a prime field. [Construction](lean/ConstructiveUpper.lean) · [Profile roots](lean/ProfileRoots.lean) · [Rank replay](verification/rank.txt)
- The priority search covered Hsu and Xu and citing or closely related papers on head complexity, softmax expressivity, VC dimension, algebraic width bounds for networks, polynomial thresholds, and restriction maps on rational curves. The team found earlier sign-counting methods and the source's upper constructions. It found no prior result giving the joint worst-case value-dimension order for this architecture. External scholarly peer review is pending. [Prior-work audit](verification/prior-work.md)
