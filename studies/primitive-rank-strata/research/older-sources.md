## Earlier primary work and corrections

- I checked the official full text of Kászonyi–Katsura (1997), pp. 1–11. Its Corollaries 2 and 3 and subsequent $E(\Pi)$ definition require nonzero box differences in suitable noncrossing configurations. It proves context-freeness for the whole slice with at most three distinct prime factors. The certificate sets allow higher-order differences and do not identify the 2010 exact strata or give an equivalent negative result. Its conclusion calls for systematic study of boxes and differences. [Official 1997 PDF](https://publi.math.unideb.hu/load_doc.php?p=376&t=pap).
- The two 1999 papers are distinct. The Debrecen supplement article, 54 suppl., pp. 885–894, gives the sufficient reciprocal bound $\sum 1/p_i<4/5$ for context-freeness of the whole slice. I checked the abstract and corroboration in the cited theorem in Kászonyi (2002), his 2000 paper, and the historical note in the monograph. The publicly indexed nine-page author scan has no text layer and the direct download returned HTTP 403. I could not check the supplement proof page by page. ## Why this is more than a wording fix

Definition 10, the classification motivation, the sentence on p. 328, the argument in Lemma 11 and the author's own Example 2 all refer to exact strata. I replayed the example from its printed vector at $n=30$. Each of the three first-difference families is nonzero somewhere. Among the second-difference families only the $\{2,5\}$ family is nonzero. The top third difference vanishes identically. This confirms the existential reading at the prescribed rank and the intentional vanishing of higher differences. The symmetric new witnesses satisfy either reading, so fixing the quantifier does not recover the conjecture. [2010 paper, pp. 325 and 328–329](https://publi.math.unideb.hu/paper/1506/download/10_5486_PMD_2010_4680.pdf#page=16).

A weaker sufficient-condition statement is close to what the rank-one proof uses. Ambient box-inequality sets can contain a rank-one vector while allowing higher-rank vectors. For example the vectors with some nonzero rank-$\alpha$ box difference form a finite union of stratified nonzero-box sets by the established Flip-Flop lemma. This supplies no exact presentation once higher-rank equalities and primitivity are imposed. Correcting the wording to use ambient covers would give up the exact classification. The new obstruction answers the printed conjecture and corrects a theorem component, with academic significance for a specific approach and family. No prize for this exact question was documented, and the whole-language questions remain separate.
