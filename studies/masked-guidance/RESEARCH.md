Their 12 June 2025 preprint analyzes dimensions one and two and poses Conjecture D.1. The ICLR 2026 version retains the question as Conjecture E.1 (page 24). Proposition 3.3(2) (page 6) and Remark 3.5 (page 7) specify a testable prediction for shared support. The source defines the two-dimensional regions explicitly. A later independent survey discussed the class-overlap prediction in its treatment of discrete guidance. ## Research and review

1. A generative-AI scout located the human conjecture and derived the diagonal class construction. A separate reviewer worked directly from the exact forward and reverse rates and also constructed a different ternary-table counterexample. The coordinator evaluated the original two-dimensional endpoint formula and each raw rate. Both calculations agree.
2. The group identified the conditional normalization within a fiber as the obstruction to an ordering based purely on support. Exact enumeration tested finite strength, both reveal orders in dimension two, the higher-dimensional example, and the stochastic normalization at every step.
3. The coordinator and independent reviewers developed an independent-coordinate target whose overlap starts arbitrarily close to zero. The proof uses a uniquely informative first reveal whose entire cylinder is shared. A competing construction on a ternary square makes both class supports proper even within the unmasked alphabet.
4. The selection proof was extended from one reveal to the leading coefficient and decay exponent of every endpoint, including tied optimal paths. The coordinator derived a subset dynamic program and checked its exact rational outputs against exhaustive enumeration.
5. An agent checked the completed manuscript against the source assumptions and independently recomputed the numerical examples. Lean, its module checker and the Rust kernel nanoda were run separately. The results of the program and scope of each check are in the verification register. The primary source and historical status were reviewed separately from kernel acceptance. [Verification](VERIFY.md)

The null hypothesis we tried to rescue was that class-private words must dominate if their full posterior equals one. This overlooks the mass needed to reach a private word along feasible prefixes. Another possible objection was that the diagonal target forced excessive correlation. The product construction removes it. A further objection concerned empty competitor-only support: both classes have private words in the original diagonal witness and the ternary product variant. Replacing joint rate normalization by per-coordinate normalization or by terminal reward tilting changes the studied process. These are separate research questions.

Human assessment of significance and priority remains open. |
