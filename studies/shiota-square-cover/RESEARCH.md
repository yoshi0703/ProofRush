All dates below are 27 November 2026. UTC times refer to saved local artifacts and verification runs. Work overlapped across agents and other research questions, so elapsed wall time is not a measurement of human or processor hours.

## Independent checks

The finite stencil checker evaluates the 121 anchors in the central collar of each of five configurations and the 28 residue/offset combinations of the periodic wire. The whole-drawing program tested 144 drawings with a total of 1,432 original edges and 487,545 emitted unit edges, counting each drawing separately. Three direct checks passed raw target-incidence clauses to a SAT solver and compared the result to a brute-force optimum on the source graph. Three deliberately invalid drawings were rejected. The five-cycle example in the reproduction instructions gives 2,073 target edges and exact budget 413. Another reviewer separately traced 1,206 complete routes recovered from 100 seeded drawings. The proof of the universal claim comes from the reduction and the general verification.
