# Research candidates

35 distinct candidates were compared across enumerative combinatorics, graph theory, arithmetic and discrete dynamics. The table ranks them after the first round of prior-art eliminations. Proposals with known solutions are recorded separately in the research record. Each source gives the original definitions, index conventions and attribution. The year is that of the cited proposal or identified source where the first date was unavailable.

Scores are judgments on a 1–5 scale, with 5 most favorable. N measures evidence that an answer would be new, S estimates tractability, X measures ease of finding or checking a counterexample, L estimates ease of a complete Lean proof and V estimates mathematical value. Rank uses 3N + 3S + X + 2L + V. Ties retain the scouting order. Costs estimate a useful first attempt on one laptop, without paid services. S means seconds to minutes, M minutes to hours, and L means a complete answer has no credible local time bound. These are estimates, not runtime measurements.

For the reserve candidates, an author’s conjecture label or a formal catalogue’s open tag is evidence of a proposal, not independent clearance of priority. The few candidates investigated in detail have a separate disposition in the research record. Recent factoring and matrix results motivated some reductions. None supplies an automatic solution to a primality-pattern or geometric existence problem.
